Pupusa Dough:

  • 3 cups (11.8 ounces/334g) masa harina , (such as maseca)
  • 2 teaspoons kosher salt
  • 2 3/4 cup cold water, (from the faucet is fine)

Filling:(any of your choice 2 cups (7.5 ounces))

  • Shredded mozzarella, (or Oaxacan cheese or any other melty cheese)
  • Braised meats. You could braise a pork shoulder, beef, chicken
  • Pureed Beans.
  • Veggies. Roasted squash,puree cauliflower in a food processor, squash, zucchini, etc.
